WHAT PLAYING THEM OUT SHOWED
Compare carries the whole flow Every door spends most of its screens getting to one place. Compare is where the work happens and the only screen that needs real depth — the earlier screens are just narrowing.
The two-screen doors are the ones worth promoting Flag, resource card and overview item land on a drafted candidate. They're the fastest path from noticing something to proving a fix, and they're the doors most likely to be used repeatedly.
Door 5 needs its detour designed, not hidden Optimizing for a metric that isn't attached means attaching it and grading a run before anything can be compared. That's a real wait, and pretending otherwise makes the session feel broken.
