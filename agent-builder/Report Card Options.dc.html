THE ONE DISAGREEMENT WORTH SETTLING You’re right that verdict + chips are an overview before drilling down — 1b doesn’t argue with that, it argues that the overview should be one layer, not four. In 1a, evaluations appear in the verdict line, the chips row, the tile, and the scope strip’s safety mini — four representations of one fact, all visible at rest. In 1b the four dimensions are the overview, and each one opens its own detail in the same place. The chips still exist; they’re what Evaluations opens into.
